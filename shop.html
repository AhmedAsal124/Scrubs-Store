Our Philosophy

Crafting Dignity & Ease for Egypt's Medical Heroes

AZ Scrubs was founded on a simple observation: medical professionals in Egypt endure grueling 12- to 24-hour shifts, yet they are often left with stiff, unbreathable scrubs that lose their shape after repeated autoclaving.

We developed a proprietary fabric blend combining medical-grade polyester durability, soft combed cotton breathability, and pure elastane stretch. Designed to move with you seamlessly through surgery, emergency rounds, and long clinic hours, every piece is backed by our full 14-day size replacement guarantee and delivered straight to all 27 Egyptian governorates.
